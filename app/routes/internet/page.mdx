# The Internet

The internet is a network of networks: computers using shared protocols to exchange information across the world.

## How it works

When you visit a site, your device asks DNS for its address, connects to the server, and requests the page. That request can travel through many independent networks before a response returns.

## Built for connection

Open standards let people publish, communicate, and build new services without needing permission from a single owner. That openness is what makes the internet useful—and worth protecting.
